# 📁 My Projects

A collection of my hands-on Arduino and embedded electronics projects. Each one below links to a folder containing the **full source code** and a **demo video**.

<br/>

## ✅ Completed

### 1. [Joystick-Controlled RGB LED Sequencer](./code/joystick-led-sequencer)
A joystick-based light sequencer. Pushing the joystick left or right steps forward or backward through three LEDs (Green, Blue, Yellow), lighting exactly one at a time based on the analog X-axis reading.

### 2. [Fire & Sound Alert System](./code/fire-sound-alert-system)
A safety monitoring system built around a flame sensor and a sound sensor. It displays live status on a 16x2 LCD and triggers a buzzer alert when flame or a loud sound is detected, with fire treated as the top priority.

### 3. [LCD Stopwatch / Timer](./code/lcd-stopwatch)
A digital stopwatch built entirely with Arduino's internal `millis()` timing (no RTC module). Displays minutes, seconds, and centiseconds on a 16x2 LCD, controlled with Start/Pause and Reset buttons.
